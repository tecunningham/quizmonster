How the categories were checked

Artworks and monuments were individually selected using their named subjects and linked references. Fashion images have captions dating the clothing to the stated decade. Elephant selections have explicit species descriptions; category membership alone was not accepted.

Some ancient monuments predate the empire associated with them; the Aztec group covers Aztec-associated sites. Cathedrals may contain later additions: labels follow their principal historical style. The image credits include a subject or date reference for every selection.
